# Artificial Intelligence Guide

A curated list of free resources and learning paths for Artificial Intelligence.

## Free Resources & Reference Links

### 1. AI Learning Resources Hub (GitHub)
Curated collection of free resources for machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and transformers.
* **Link:** [https://github.com/boudribila/AI-Learning-Resources](https://github.com/boudribila/AI-Learning-Resources)

### 2. Fast.ai - Practical Deep Learning for Coders
A hands-on, top-down course designed to get you building deep learning models as quickly as possible.
* **Link:** [https://www.fast.ai/](https://www.fast.ai/)

### 3. Elements of AI (University of Helsinki)
A series of free online courses created by Reaktor and the University of Helsinki to teach AI basics.
* **Link:** [https://www.elementsofai.com/](https://www.elementsofai.com/)

### 4. MIT OpenCourseWare - Artificial Intelligence
Lecture notes, assignments, and exams from MIT's classic undergraduate AI course.
* **Link:** [https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-034-artificial-intelligence-fall-2010/](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-034-artificial-intelligence-fall-2010/)
