# Computer Networking Guide

A curated list of free resources and learning paths for Computer Networking.

## Free Resources & Reference Links

### 1. FreeBookCentre Networking Ebooks
Collection of open university lecture notes and free textbooks covering TCP/IP, OSI models, and routing.
* **Link:** [https://www.freebookcentre.net/Networking/Free-Computer-Networking-Books-Download.html](https://www.freebookcentre.net/Networking/Free-Computer-Networking-Books-Download.html)

### 2. Cisco Networking Basics
Introductory networking concepts, subnetting, hardware basics, and architecture guides for beginners.
* **Link:** [https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/solutions/small-business/resource-center/networking/networking-basics.html](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/solutions/small-business/resource-center/networking/networking-basics.html)

### 3. Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach (Online Resources)
Interactive applets, Wireshark labs, and video lectures supplementing the standard networking textbook.
* **Link:** [https://gaia.cs.umass.edu/kurose_ross/](https://gaia.cs.umass.edu/kurose_ross/)

### 4. Cloudflare Learning Center - Networking
Easy-to-understand articles explaining DNS, IP addresses, BGP, protocols, and how the internet works.
* **Link:** [https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/network-layer/what-is-a-network/](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/network-layer/what-is-a-network/)
